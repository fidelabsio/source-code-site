Merchant-facing, click-by-click walkthroughs for each feature of the app, as seen inside the Shopify Admin. Screenshots from the live admin UI are referenced inline in the original documentation source; they are omitted from this PDF and marked with a screenshot placeholder — the numbered steps below each one describe exactly what that screenshot shows.

## Hide Price
#### If you don't want customers to see product pricing:
1. Open **Apps → Select your APP → Quote Settings**.
2. Scroll to the **Hide Price** section.
3. Choose where prices should be hidden:
	- All products
	- Specific collections
	- Hand-picked products
4. Select the pages where prices will be hidden.
5. Hide the **Add to Cart** and **Buy Now** buttons (Optional).
6. Choose whether to display replacement text when prices are hidden.
7. Click **Save**.

## Request Quote
#### 1. General Settings — configure where and how the Request Quote button appears on your storefront.
1. Open **Apps → Select your APP → Quote Settings**.
2. Select where the Request Quote button should appear.
3. Choose how the button behaves.
4. Configure customer visibility if required.
![](screenshot)
> 💡 **Tip:** Use **All Products** to enable the Request Quote button across your entire store.
#### 2. Button Style — customize the appearance of the Request Quote button to match your store's branding.
1. Enter the button label.
2. Choose the text and background colours.
3. Adjust the text size.
4. Save your changes.
![](screenshot)
#### 3. Quote Widget — configure the floating Quote Widget displayed on your storefront.
1. Enable the Quote Widget.
2. Set the widget label.
3. Choose its position.
4. Customize the widget colours.
![](screenshot)
#### 4. Toast Notifications — customize the notification messages displayed after customer actions.
1. Enable toast notifications.
2. Edit the notification messages.
3. Customize the appearance.
4. Select the notification position.
![](screenshot)

## Quote Cart
#### 1. View Quote Details — review all information submitted with a customer's quote request.
1. Open **Apps → Select your APP → Dashboard**.
2. From the **Recent Quotes** section, click **View all** to open the Quote List.
![](screenshot)
**OR** Click **Quote List** from the left sidebar.
![](screenshot)
1. Open a quote from the **Quote List**.
#### 2. Edit Customer Information — update customer and shipping information before responding to the quote.
1. From the Quote Details page, click **Edit** in the Customer section.
![](screenshot)
1. Update the customer information or shipping address as required.
![](screenshot)
1. Click **Done** to finish editing.
#### 3. Save Quote Changes — save any updates made to the quote before responding to the customer.
1. Review the updated quote information.
2. Click **Save**.
![](screenshot)
1. Verify the confirmation message that the quote has been saved successfully.
![](screenshot)
#### 4. Respond to the Quote — accept or reject the quote after reviewing and updating the details.
1. Click **Accept** or **Reject**.
![](screenshot)
1. Choose the option as required.
![](screenshot)

## Quote Form Builder
#### 1. Configure Quote Form — customize the Request Quote form shown to customers before they submit a quote request.
1. Open **Apps → Select your APP → Quote Settings → Form Builder**.
2. Edit the form heading and submit button text.
3. Customize the button style.
4. Choose which product details to display.
5. Add or remove customer information fields.
6. Save your changes.
![](screenshot)
#### 2. Configure Contact Fields — configure the customer information fields displayed in the Request Quote form.
1. Scroll down to the **Form Builder**.
2. Expand a contact field to edit its settings.
3. Update the field label, placeholder, and help text if needed.
4. Mark the field as **Required** or optional.
5. Add new fields or remove existing ones.
6. Save your changes.
![](screenshot)
> 💡 **Tip:** Only keep the fields you need to make the quote request process simple for your customers.

## Dashboard Overview
#### The Dashboard provides a quick overview of your quote activity, recent requests, and key performance metrics.
1. Open **Apps → Your APP**.
2. The **Dashboard** opens automatically after launching the app.
3. Review the setup guide to complete the initial configuration.
4. Check the summary cards for:
	- **Total Quotes**
	- **This Month**
	- **Accepted Quotes**
	- **Revenue**
5. View the **Recent Quotes** section to monitor incoming quote requests.
6. Review **Top Requested Products** and the **Summary** panel for quick insights.
![](screenshot)
> 💡 **Tip:** Use the dashboard to monitor quote activity and quickly access recent customer requests.

## Email Notifications
#### 1. Settings page — configure the email provider and manage notification templates.
1. Open **Apps → Select your APP → Settings → Email Settings**.
2. Choose your preferred email provider.
3. Enter the admin notification email address.
4. Click **Save**.
5. Enable or edit the required email notifications.
![](screenshot)
> 💡 **Tip:** Enable only the notifications you want customers or administrators to receive.
#### 2. Email Template — customize the content and appearance of each email notification.
1. Open the email template you want to edit.
2. Enable or disable the notification.
3. Update the email subject and heading.
4. Customize the email content and action button.
5. Send a test email to verify the template.
6. Save your changes.
![](screenshot)
> 💡 **Tip:** Use the preview panel to review your email before sending it to customers.
