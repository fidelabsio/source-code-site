## 3. Reading Order
### Path A — your own team implements
```text
LICENSE.md → ONBOARDING.md → Installation Guide → White Label Guide
    → Deployment Guide → QUICK-START-CHECKLIST.md → UPGRADE.md
```
Total reading time ≈ 100 minutes. Skip nothing.
### Path B — an implementation partner does the work
**You read:** `LICENSE.md` → `ONBOARDING.md` §2, §3 (Path B), §8 → `QUICK-START-CHECKLIST.md` Phases 0, 8, 9
**Your partner reads:** Installation Guide → White Label Guide → Deployment Guide → FAQ
The licence obligations stay with you, the licence holder — not with your partner. Do not delegate §2 or Phase 8.
